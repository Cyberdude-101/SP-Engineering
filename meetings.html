What a meeting looks like

An hour, and something to show for it

  1. Turn up

    Room 105 straight after school. Sign the attendance sheet on your way in — that is the entire membership process.

  2. Get the problem

    A challenge and a pile of materials. Usually you work in a group of two or three, and you pick your own approach.

  3. Build and test it

    Most units run across a few meetings and finish with a test. Some things hold. Some things very much do not.
