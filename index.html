What we build

Four weeks of theory is worth one afternoon of it breaking

We try to cover as much of engineering as we can fit into an hour after school. Every unit ends with a test — sometimes that means a stopwatch, sometimes it means finding out how much weight a bridge takes before the glue lets go.

Planes and flight

Why a wing lifts, where the centre of gravity has to sit, and what happens when it doesn’t. Then we build gliders and throw them down the hallway.

Electronics and circuitry

Current, resistance, and why the LED needs a resistor in front of it. Breadboards, multimeters, and the occasional smell of something burning.

Getting in

There is no sign-up process

Turn up to a meeting and write your name on the attendance sheet. That is genuinely the whole thing — no form, no fee, and it doesn’t matter if you’ve missed every meeting so far.
