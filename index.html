What we do

Engineering is a wide subject and we try to cover as much of it as fits into an hour after school. Some units are hands-on, some are on a screen, and most run over a few meetings.

Code and websites

HTML, CSS and a bit of JavaScript. This site is one of the club’s own projects — if software is your thing, there is plenty here to work on.

Electronics and circuitry

Current, resistance, and why an LED needs a resistor in front of it. Breadboards and multimeters — one of the things we’re getting into this year.

Building and testing

How a wing makes lift, what holds a bridge up, and what happens when it doesn’t. We build things and then find out how they hold up.

Joining

Come to a meeting and write your name on the attendance sheet. There’s no form and no fee, and it doesn’t matter if you’ve missed the earlier meetings.
