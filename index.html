Engineering Club

We meet every other Wednesday in Room 105 and build things — bridges, gliders, circuits, 3D prints — then test them to see how they hold up. Anyone can come.

What we build

We try to cover as much of engineering as fits into an hour after school. Most units run over a few meetings and end with some kind of test.

Planes and flight

How a wing makes lift, where the center of gravity needs to be, and what happens when it isn’t. We build gliders and test them in the hallway.

Electronics and circuitry

Current, resistance, and why an LED needs a resistor in front of it. Mostly breadboards and multimeters.

Joining

Come to a meeting and write your name on the attendance sheet. There’s no form and no fee, and it doesn’t matter if you’ve missed the earlier meetings.
