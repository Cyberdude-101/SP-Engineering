3:30–4:30 PM  ·  Mrs. Best’s room (#105)

  1. Come to Room 105

    Straight after school on a meeting Wednesday. Mrs. Best’s room. You don’t need to tell anyone you’re coming.

  2. Sign the sheet

    Write your name on the attendance paper by the door. There’s no form to fill in.

  3. Start building

    You’ll be in a group within about ten minutes. Materials are already on the desks.

Common questions

Do I need to be good at math?

No. Most of what we do is hands-on — cutting, gluing, taping, testing. If a unit needs a calculation, someone will walk you through it.

What if I miss meetings?

That’s fine. Units run over a few sessions, but groups absorb people easily and plenty of members come to about half of them.

Do I need to bring anything?

No. Materials are provided and it’s free.

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes, any time. There is no cut-off and no catching up to do.

Who runs it

Mrs. Best is the club’s teacher sponsor. Questions from students or parents are welcome.
