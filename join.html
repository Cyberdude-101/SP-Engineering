Common questions

Do I need to be good at math?

No. We don’t do much complicated math, and there are always people there to help if you get stuck.

What if I miss meetings?

That’s fine. Lots of members miss a few, and it’s easy to catch back up with your group.

Do I need to bring anything?

No, we provide everything. There’s a $5 fee to join for the year. If you’d rather use your own laptop for coding, you can bring one.

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes, you can join at any point in the year.

Who runs it

Mrs. Best is the club’s teacher sponsor. Questions from students or parents are welcome.
